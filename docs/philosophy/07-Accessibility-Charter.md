# Architect's Intent

Everyone is welcome. Our responsibility is to make sure everyone can participate.

We believe accessibility is an expression of respect.

Every person deserves to participate with confidence,
independence,
and dignity.

Our responsibility is not to create special experiences for some people.

Our responsibility is to create one thoughtful experience that welcomes everyone.

# Accessibility Doctrine

## Inclusion Before Compliance

Accessibility is not an obligation imposed by regulations.

It is a natural expression of respect for people.

We build experiences that welcome everyone because inclusion reflects who we are—not because standards require it.

Compliance may define the minimum.

Our values define our goal.

# What is Accessibility?

Accessibility is the practice of ensuring that every person can participate with confidence, independence, and dignity.

It is not a feature.

It is not an enhancement.

It is a commitment that every experience we create is intentionally inclusive.

When we design for inclusion, everyone benefits.

**Inclusion is not an accessibility feature. It is the character of our product.**

# Inclusion Principles

If someone cannot use our product,
our first question should never be:

"Why can't they use it?"

It should be:

"What barrier did we create?"

# Accessibility Decision Framework
Before introducing a feature, ask:

Does everyone have an opportunity to use it?

↓

Can people complete it independently?

↓

Does it preserve dignity?

↓

Does it align with our previous charters?

↓

Have we removed unnecessary barriers?


---
# Inclusion Review

Before releasing a feature, ask:

✓ Can everyone participate?

✓ Can people succeed independently?

✓ Have we removed unnecessary barriers?

✓ Does this experience preserve dignity?

✓ Would we proudly share this experience with our own family?

If yes,

the experience is ready.

---

# Closing Statement
Accessibility is not about meeting standards.

It is about meeting people where they are.

Every thoughtful decision removes a barrier.

Every removed barrier creates an opportunity.

Every opportunity allows another person to participate with confidence,
independence,
and dignity.

That is the promise of Project ButterFly.
