# 🦋 Project Constitution

> **The Permanent Foundation of Project ButterFly**

The Constitution is the highest authority within Project ButterFly.

It defines the enduring principles that guide every decision made throughout the methodology.

While technologies evolve, engineering practices mature, and products grow, the Constitution remains intentionally stable.

It is the North Star of Project ButterFly.

---

# The Question It Answers

The Constitution answers one timeless question:

> **What principles should never change?**

Every Charter, Standard, Guide, Reference Implementation, and future contribution must align with these principles.

If a conflict arises, the Constitution takes precedence.

---

# Relationship to the Methodology

Project ButterFly is intentionally organized as a progressive engineering methodology.

```text
Constitution
        ↓
Charters
        ↓
Standards
        ↓
Guides
        ↓
Reference Implementations
```

Each layer builds upon the previous one.

The Constitution provides the foundation upon which every other document is built.

---

# Characteristics of the Constitution

The Constitution is intentionally:

- Timeless
- Technology-agnostic
- Principle-driven
- Memorable
- Stable
- Concise

Its purpose is not to prescribe implementation details.

Its purpose is to protect the identity of Project ButterFly.

---

# The Doctrines

## Purpose Before Code

Every meaningful product begins with purpose.

Technology should always serve intention, never replace it.

---

## Comfort Before Conversion

People are more important than metrics.

Every interaction should respect the user's comfort, trust, and well-being.

---

## Confidence Before Completion

A product is complete only when it inspires confidence.

Quality should never be sacrificed simply to finish sooner.

---

## Calm is Beautiful

Simplicity creates clarity.

Thoughtful design removes unnecessary complexity and helps people focus on what matters.

---

## Every Feature Must Earn Its Place

Every addition should provide meaningful value.

Complexity should never exist without purpose.

---

## Inclusion Before Compliance

Accessibility is about people—not checklists.

Everyone deserves equal opportunity to use and benefit from technology.

---

## Engineering Serves Experience

Technology is a means, not an end.

Engineering exists to create reliable, meaningful, and enjoyable experiences.

---

## Every Module Tells One Story

Each module should have one clear responsibility.

Focused systems are easier to understand, maintain, and evolve.

---

## Consistency Creates Confidence

Consistency reduces uncertainty.

Predictable experiences help people learn, trust, and use products with confidence.

---

## Every Component Serves One Purpose

Reusable components should remain focused, understandable, and intentional.

Simple components create stronger systems.

---

# Closing Statement

Technologies change.

Frameworks evolve.

Products grow.

Principles endure.

Every journey through Project ButterFly begins with the Constitution because lasting engineering is built upon lasting principles.
